## Brand & Style

This design system powers a high-velocity digital asset treasury and global liquidity platform tailored for modern web viewports and high-density mobile displays. The aesthetic balances deep-space institutional security with the luminous kinetic energy of atmospheric auroras. 

To convey precision, uncompromised performance, and modern institutional rigor, the design system strips away decorative ink traps, quirky apertures, and visual clutter. In their place, it establishes an austere, hyper-crisp geometric structure. Surfaces mimic precision-cut obsidian slabs and edge-lit optical glass floating over dark abyssal substrates (`#050811` and `#061B18`), highlighted by concentrated bursts of aurora cyan and hyper-saturated indigo. 

The emotional tone is calm, sovereign, and technologically unyielding—delivering an institutional-grade fintech cockpit where every pixel, border, and metric is purpose-built for ultra-legible financial execution.

## Colors

The system operates strictly within a dedicated dark color mode optimized for OLED efficiency and extreme contrast. Chromatic energy is reserved strictly for actionable targets, market trajectories, and critical financial indicators.

### Surface and Ground Architecture
- **Void Ground (`background-void` / `#050811`)**: Deepest substrate level, offering a zero-light foundation for high-contrast typography.
- **Atmospheric Underlay (`background-teal` / `#061B18`)**: Injected through dynamic radial lighting or low-opacity gradient transitions behind top metrics.
- **Base Surface (`neutral_color_hex` / `#0D1424`)**: The primary structural plane for resting modules, lists, and navigation islands.
- **Active Treasury Tier (`surface-elevated` / `#0B2924`)**: Elevated surface variant reserved for high-yield vaults, active card balances, and prioritized widgets.

### Aurora Accents
- **Primary Cyan (`#35D9D0`)**: Signifies immediate actionability, active states, live network confirmations, and positive yields.
- **Secondary Indigo (`#7567F8`)**: Provides foundational depth, structural hierarchy, secondary controls, and the finishing gradient anchor for the Aurora blend.

### Semantic Tiers
- **Success (`#10B981`)**: Positive yield, inbound settlement, and nominal system state.
- **Warning (`#F59E0B`)**: Gas fee spikes, pending approvals, and temporary locks.
- **Danger (`#EF4444`)**: Liquidation thresholds, network warnings, and critical failures.

## Typography

Typography has been overhauled to eliminate idiosyncratic ink traps and stylized flairs in favor of crisp, engineered geometric clarity.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** governs all hero financial balances, section titles, modal headers, and key numerical readouts. Its pure, modern geometry retains high structural authority without introducing distracting ornamental details.
- **Inter** handles all running context, data tables, wallet addresses, transaction timelines, input labels, and status badges. It delivers transparent, uncompromised legibility across both bright accents and low-contrast surface tints.

### Numerical Discipline
All financial metrics, currency tokens, live tickers, and exchange figures must strictly enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. This prevents horizontal jitter during high-frequency balance oscillations and live order fills.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model combines an ergonomic mobile-first structural envelope with a strict 8px/4px coordinate system.

### Spatial Principles
- **Constraint Envelope**: Mobile viewports are constrained to a focused `440px` maximum width. On wider viewports (tablets, desktops), the application centers automatically over an ambient field rendered with deep cyan-teal radial depth.
- **Screen Margins**: Baseline horizontal padding is set to `16px` (`gutter-lg`), scaling to `20px` (`gutter-xl`) on viewports wider than `390px`.
- **Rhythm Units**: All layout spacing scales in increments of `8px` (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`). The `4px` unit (`gutter-xs`) is reserved for micro-adjustments within chip labels, icon-text pairings, and stacked numeric captions.
- **Bottom Navigation Clearance**: All scroll containers enforce an absolute `96px` bottom inset (`bottom-nav-clearance`) to ensure content is never obscured by the floating navigation bar.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through optical transmission, stacked luminous planes, and hairline boundaries rather than heavy, opaque drop shadows.

- **Layer 0 (Void Ground)**: `#050811` surface accented by directional radial gradients: `radial-gradient(circle at 80% 0%, rgba(53, 217, 208, 0.10), transparent 50%)`.
- **Layer 1 (Card Foundation)**: `#0D1424` backed by a 1px border stroke using `border-hairline` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`). Diffuse elevation shadow: `0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated & Active Vaults)**: `#0B2924` surface bordered with `rgba(53, 217, 208, 0.20)` and an inner glow: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08), 0 0 20px rgba(53, 217, 208, 0.06)`.
- **Layer 3 (Floating HUDs & Sheets)**: Translucent glass panel: `rgba(13, 20, 36, 0.82)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, wrapped with `border-interactive` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs a rounded, geometric architecture that balances structural rigidity with smooth tactile touch surfaces:

- **Surface Containers & Cards**: Defined at `1.25rem` (20px) to `1.5rem` (24px) to create distinct, modern chassis boundaries.
- **Action Buttons & Badges**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to guarantee immediate touch affordance and separate interactive controls from structural containers.
- **Form Controls & Inputs**: Form fields maintain a consistent `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) curvature.
- **Bottom Sheets**: Modals and drawer panels utilize top-corner radii of `1.75rem` (28px).
