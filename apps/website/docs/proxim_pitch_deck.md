## SLIDE 2: The Problem

### Cross-Border Money Movement in Emerging Markets is Broken

1. **Predatory Traditional Banking Fees & Delays**
   * Legacy correspondent banking (SWIFT) charges **5% to 12%** in fees and takes **3 to 7 business days** to clear supplier payments, contractor salaries, and remittances.
2. **Severe FX Shortages & Capital Controls**
   * Businesses and importers face strict foreign currency limits, blocking critical cross-border trade.
3. **P2P Crypto Risks & Escrow Freezes**
   * Converting crypto to local currency via P2P exchanges exposes users to scams, counterparties with tainted funds, frozen bank accounts, and 15–45 minute escrow wait times.
4. **Web3 Usability Friction**
   * Everyday merchants and consumers cannot navigate network gas tokens, seed phrases, or complex cross-chain bridges.
