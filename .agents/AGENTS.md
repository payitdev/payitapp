# Proxim Project Rules & Style Guide

## Product Positioning & Invisible Crypto Principle
- Proxim is a modern, premium payments app for sending, receiving, holding, converting, and managing money across currencies.
- Infrastructure (crypto, Web3, blockchain, KMS, settlement layers) MUST remain invisible to standard users.
- NEVER expose technical jargon in default flows: `Blockchain`, `Wallet Address`, `Gas Fee`, `Network`, `Token`, `Bridge`, `RPC`, `Smart Contract`, `Layer 2`, `Private Key`, `Seed Phrase`, `Chain`, `Mainnet`.
- Standard terms:
  - "Send Money" (not "Execute transaction")
  - "Who are you paying?" (not "Beneficiary")
  - "Money" or "Balance" (not "Wallet")
  - "Where should we send it?" (not "Recipient Address")
  - "You're signed in." (not "Authentication Successful")
  - "Verify your identity to unlock more features." (not "KYC Pending")

## Dashboard & Activity Copy Rules
- Balance reads: "Available" `₦420,000` (Never separate by blockchain network or wallet ID).
- Activity feed reads like a banking app: "Sent to David" `₦25,000`, "Received from Sarah" `$200`, "Payroll received" `₦180,000`.
- Tone: Calm, confident, helpful, and premium ("Money sent.", "Payment received.", "You're all set.", "Ready when you are.").
- NEVER use hype language: "Success!", "Awesome!", "Congratulations!", "Boom!".
- Errors must be plain and helpful ("We couldn't complete your payment. Please try again.").
